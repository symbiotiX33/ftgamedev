Update on Chain System, Shadow Work, and First Major Boss

05.08.2026

We have completely revamped the chain system between characters, improved level lighting, and started work on a new boss.

The chain now feels much smoother and more natural. We have revised the physics of its tension and interaction with the environment, making the gameplay more responsive.

Besides that, lighting and shadows have been improved, making the levels more atmospheric and enjoyable to explore.

The team is currently actively working on developing the first major boss, which will present a serious challenge and will require skillful teamwork between the two characters.
